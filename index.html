How to play

Read the dialogue and choose the best answer. If you make a mistake, Ash the cat nudges you and explains the rule — and you can try again.

In clue scenes, move the magnifying glass over the picture and click on suspicious things.

The notebook keeps your clues. The globe button shows the Russian translation of the current line.
